Four elements, one column, left-aligned, all inside an 8% safe margin. Anything beyond these four is a cut. Pack A tiles already follow this; Pack B tiles need re-laying out to match.

  1. Accent bar. Short mint rule, top-left of the text column. It anchors the eye and is the only decoration.
  2. Headline. Montserrat SemiBold, sentence case, 2–3 lines, eight words or fewer. The closing word or phrase is set in mint: that is the payoff, so the headline must be written to land on it. One idea only.
  3. Support line. One line, six words max, regular weight, white. A fragment is fine. It sharpens the headline; it never explains it. Drop it entirely if the headline already carries the idea.
  4. Footer row. Sign-off on the left (Your fund with super powers for Pack B, Finance is next. Tokenisation is how. for Pack A) and the tokeniser wordmark on the right, on the same baseline, small. The wordmark never sits on its own line.

Type scale. Headline ~9% of tile width, support ~3%, footer ~2.8%. Photography under the slate wash so white type holds contrast everywhere in the text column.

Not allowed. A sign-off in mint stacked above a wordmark on its own line. Support lines that run to two lines. Two bold weights. Kicker lines (dates, labels) unless they replace the support line. More than one mint phrase.
